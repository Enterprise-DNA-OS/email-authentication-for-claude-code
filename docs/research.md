# Target research

Checked 4 November 2026. New family: email-authentication. No matching category or DMARC.global target existed in the rebuild registry. The finished reference was Media Relations for Claude Code.

DMARC.global publishes US$14,990 per year for its 1,000-domain MSP tier, or US$1,499 per month. This is a list price for a high-volume portfolio, not a verified New Zealand or Australian customer invoice. Smaller tiers cost less and are not the five-figure target. [Vendor pricing](https://dmarc.global/pricing).

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Annual bill | 2 | Published five-figure tier, below the 20k scoring anchor |
| Data-shaped | 5 | Domains, report observations, reviews and investigations |
| Our buyers pay for it | 2 | Potential fit for IT service firms. No confirmed purchase found in local customer or CRM-reference snapshots. No live CRM query was made. |
| Search demand | 2 | General alternatives discussions and comparison pages exist. Brand-specific volume was not measured. |
| One-session buildable | 4 | Report ingestion and operator review work locally. Collection and DNS hosting remain separate. |
| Import path | 3 | Standard receiver XML is documented. Native vendor history export was not verified. |

Total: 18/30. Dromo scored 20/30 but its published base subscription fails the five-figure filter. ReturnGO scored 19/30 and also fails that filter at its published Pro starting price. Their enterprise prices are private, so no large bill was invented. [Dromo pricing](https://dromo.io/pricing). [ReturnGO pricing](https://returngo.ai/pricing/).

The vendor's [analysis page](https://dmarc.global/dmarc-report-analysis) and [MSP page](https://dmarc.global/email-authentication-for-msps) describe aggregate reports, portfolio management and branded reports. This implementation owns the report-review workflow. It does not reproduce vendor hosting, DNS automation, identity controls or a report receiver.

Valimail and dmarcian are family rivals, not new-family queue candidates. Valimail documents a [Domains CSV report](https://support.valimail.com/en/articles/13657118-valimail-enforce-domains-report), but this repo does not claim to import it. The comparison page makes no claim that a vendor cannot answer a specific question. Its ten questions are proven against this implementation instead.
